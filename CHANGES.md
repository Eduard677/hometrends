## Phase 1 — Metadata and structured data

One shared `pageHead()` in `src/lib/seo.ts`, used by all 16 route heads.
Adds per-page `og:title` (title minus site suffix), `og:description`,
`og:type`, `og:url`, `og:site_name`, `og:locale=en_IE`, absolute `og:image`
with width/height/alt, `twitter:title|description|image`, and `canonical`.

Meta descriptions no longer prefix the product name that already leads the
`<title>`, and no longer slice to a fixed character budget that cut
mid-sentence. They cut on a sentence boundary where one survives, else a word
boundary, capped at 155 including the suffix. Audited example is 124 chars.

JSON-LD gained `geo` and `sameAs` on the business, `sku` and `itemCondition`
on the product, and `BreadcrumbList` on PDPs and collection pages. No
`AggregateRating` is authored, deliberately — self-declaring the 4.9/94 is a
manual-action risk.

## Crawlability

- `sitemap.xml`: 771 urls, previously **zero** `<lastmod>`. Every entry now
  carries one, stamped from `ht-shop.json`'s mtime rather than build time — a
  build stamp would tell crawlers the whole site changed on every deploy.
- `robots.txt` already existed and already referenced the sitemap. Unchanged.
- Pagination was `useState` + `<button>`: pages 2–31 had **no URL**, were
  invisible to crawlers and did nothing with JS disabled. Now driven by a
  `?page=` search param on `/shop` and `/collections/$slug`, with real anchors
  carrying `rel="prev"`/`rel="next"`. Verified on `/shop?page=2`.
- Paginated pages **self-canonicalise** (`/shop?page=2` → itself) and carry a
  "page N" title suffix.
- `offers.availability` now emitted. **Correction to the Phase 0 note:**
  ht-shop.json's `availability` *string* is empty on all 742 products, but the
  `inStock` *boolean* is populated (727 true / 15 false), so InStock and
  OutOfStock are real data. Products without the flag get no claim.

## Judgement calls

**The two "living room" collections are disjoint.** `living-room` is 70
products (category "Living Room" — tables, media units, bookcases);
`sofas-chairs` is 78 (38 "Sofas & Chairs" + 40 "Chairs & Footstools"). **Zero
products carry both tags.** The brief described them as a duplication; they
are not. The 301 was implemented as decided, which means **78 seating pieces
now redirect to a collection containing no sofas**. Flagged rather than
silently absorbed. Reversing it is a one-line change in
`collections.$slug.tsx`.

**"Sofas & Chairs" facet is hidden, not deleted.** Removing it from product
data would breach "never modify catalogue content", so it is filtered out of
the facet list. The 38 pieces remain reachable in unfiltered listings and
search.

**Struck prices** are now body ink at 45% opacity rather than a separate grey
— which grey depended on which of three stylesheet layers won. No red and no
badge existed to remove.

**Meta suffix says "Order from", not "buy online".** The cart model is
settled, but this build's checkout is an interstitial that takes no payment;
"buy online" would overstate what the site does.

**PDP gallery `srcset` deliberately not built.** Measured at 390px: 12 `<img>`
but **6 unique URLs and 6 fetches** — slides and thumbnails share URLs, so the
browser downloads each photograph once. Thumbnails are also `display:none`
below 900px. Giving them their own `srcset` would mean generating ~2900
derivative files for a decode saving and **no bandwidth saving**.

### Legibility does not depend on the image

A dark-to-transparent wash runs from the left edge, strongest behind the copy
and fully clear by ~72% across, so the subject on the right is never dimmed.
This means the headline stays legible whatever photograph lands in the slot.

**Contrast measured against the composited pixels**, not estimated — the
overlay was strengthened once after the first attempt fell short:

| Element | Size | Ratio | Required | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `h1` | 64px large | **5.53:1** | 3:1 | PASS |
| standfirst `p` | 16px normal | **6.24:1** | 4.5:1 | PASS |
| primary button | 16px normal | **13.16:1** | 4.5:1 | PASS |

First attempt measured 3.54:1 on the headline — passing for large text but
leaving the normal-size standfirst under 4.5:1. Recorded because the margin
matters if the wash is ever lightened for aesthetic reasons.

**Judgement call:** the placeholder's `alt` is empty rather than describing a
photograph that does not exist. The previous alt described a dining table,
sofa and lit fire; leaving it in place would have described the old image to a
screen reader while a flat placeholder was on screen.
